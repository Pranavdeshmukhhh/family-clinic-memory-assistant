Memory: Hindsight
"7 PM, 40 patients waiting. Will the doctor remember this patient's penicillin allergy? Hindsight does."
Without persistent memory, every visit starts from scratch — the AI has no context about allergies, past prescriptions, or chronic conditions. With Hindsight, each visit builds on the last: allergy warnings fire automatically, drug interactions are caught, and the doctor gets a personalized recommendation in seconds. More visits = smarter answers.
